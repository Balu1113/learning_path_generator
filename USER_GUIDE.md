## Getting Started

### Creating an Account
1. Click **Login** in the top right
2. Select **Create Account**
3. Enter username (3-150 characters, alphanumeric + . - _)
4. Enter password (minimum 8 characters)
5. Click **Sign Up**

### Generating Your First Learning Path

#### Quick Path (Fastest)
1. Go to **Quick Path** page
2. Enter your learning goal (e.g., "Learn Python for Data Science")
3. Select your current level: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced
4. Choose hours per day you can study
5. Select format preference (courses, projects, books, etc.)
6. Click **Generate**

#### Structured Path (Recommended)
1. Go to **Structured Path** page
2. Fill in the same details as Quick Path
3. Click **Generate Structured**
4. Get a detailed roadmap with phases, topics, and verified resources

#### Agent Mode (Most Detailed)
1. Go to **Agent Mode**
2. Describe your learning goal in detail
3. The AI agent will break it down into steps
4. Review the generated plan

## Core Features

### 1. Learning Paths
**What it is**: A personalized roadmap from your current level to your goal.

**How to use**:
- View all your paths in **Path History**
- Click a path to open it in the **Study Workspace**
- Share paths with others using the **Share** button
- Export paths as calendar (.ics) or Markdown

**Path Formats**:
- **Markdown**: Simple text-based roadmap
- **Structured**: Interactive checklist with phases and topics

### 2. Adaptive Placement Test
**What it is**: A smart assessment that adapts to your skill level.

**How to use**:
1. Go to **Placement Test**
2. Enter your learning goal
3. Answer questions one at a time
4. The test stops when it has enough confidence
5. Get your suggested starting level

**How it works**:
- Starts with medium difficulty
- If you answer correctly → harder questions
- If you miss → stops and suggests your level
- Typically 5-15 questions total

### 3. Phase Tests
**What it is**: Assessments for each phase of your learning path.

**How to use**:
1. In Study Workspace, click **Test** on any phase
2. Answer 5-10 multiple-choice questions
3. Get your score and percentage
4. Review missed questions
5. Optional: Take remediation quiz for missed topics

**Scoring**:
- 80%+ = Advanced mastery
- 50-79% = Intermediate understanding
- <50% = Needs more practice

### 4. Flashcards (Spaced Repetition)
**What it is**: Smart flashcards that adapt to your learning pace.

**How to use**:
1. In Study Workspace, click **Flashcards**
2. Click **Generate** to create cards for a phase
3. Click **Review** to start studying
4. Rate each card: Again, Hard, Good, or Easy
5. Cards reappear based on your ratings

**SM-2 Algorithm**:
- **Again**: Card resets, appears tomorrow
- **Hard**: Interval increases slightly
- **Good**: Interval increases normally
- **Easy**: Interval increases significantly

### 5. Study Notes
**What it is**: AI-generated deep-dive notes for each topic.

**How to use**:
1. In Study Workspace, click the **Notes** button on any topic
2. Read Level 1 (core concepts)
3. Click **Go Deeper** for Level 2 (advanced insights)
4. Continue for Level 3+ (expert details)

**Note Levels**:
- **Level 1**: Big idea, how it works, worked example
- **Level 2**: Advanced insights, edge cases, connections
- **Level 3+**: Expert nuances, performance, real-world failures

## Study Workspace

### Overview
The Study Workspace is where you do the actual learning. It's organized into:
- **Left**: Phase selector (sticky)
- **Center**: Topics and resources for current phase
- **Right**: Tools (flashcards, chat, notes, projects)

### Workflow
1. **Select a phase** from the left rail
2. **Read the phase description** in the center
3. **Study each topic**:
   - Read the topic details
   - Click resource links to learn
   - Take notes
   - Mark as done when complete
4. **Use tools** on the right:
   - **Flashcards**: Review key concepts
   - **Chat**: Ask questions about the path
   - **Notes**: Deep-dive into topics
   - **Projects**: Build capstone projects
5. **Take the phase test** when ready
6. **Move to next phase** when complete

### Progress Tracking
- **Progress bar** at top shows overall completion
- **Phase indicators** show which phases are complete
- **Topic checkboxes** track individual topics
- **Streak heatmap** shows your study consistency

### Recommendation System
The workspace shows a **recommendation banner** that tells you:
- **What to do next** based on your progress
- **Why** (e.g., "You completed Phase 1")
- **What's blocked** (e.g., "Complete Phase 2 before Phase 3")

## Assessment & Testing

### Placement Test
**Purpose**: Determine your starting level for a new goal

**Process**:
1. One question at a time
2. Adaptive difficulty (gets harder if you're correct)
3. Stops when confident in your level
4. Suggests: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced

### Phase Tests
**Purpose**: Verify you've mastered a phase

**Process**:
1. 5-10 multiple-choice questions
2. All questions at once
3. Immediate scoring
4. Review of correct/incorrect answers
5. Optional remediation for missed questions

### Remediation
**Purpose**: Re-teach topics you missed

**Process**:
1. After phase test, if you missed questions
2. Click **Review Missed Questions**
3. Answer the same questions again
4. Understand why you missed them

### Skill Mastery Tracking
Your skills are tracked as:
- **Unassessed**: No evidence yet
- **Developing**: Some evidence (test scores 50-69%)
- **Validated**: Strong evidence (test scores 70%+)

## Portfolio & Evidence

### Skill Passport
**What it is**: Your public portfolio of validated skills and achievements.

**How to access**:
1. Click your username → **Portfolio**
2. View your skills, projects, and test results
3. Click **Privacy Settings** to control what's public

### Privacy Controls
- **Is Public**: Make your portfolio visible to others
- **Show Projects**: Include shipped projects
- **Show Test Scores**: Include assessment results
- **Show Study Hours**: Include total study time

### Sharing Your Portfolio
1. Go to **Portfolio**
2. Click **Privacy Settings**
3. Toggle **Is Public** on
4. Share your profile URL: `yoursite.com/portfolio/username`

### Exporting
1. Go to **Portfolio**
2. Click **Export to Markdown**
3. Share the file with recruiters or colleagues

### Evidence Types
- **Test Results**: Phase test scores (70%+ = validated)
- **Projects**: Reviewed capstone projects
- **Study Hours**: Total time invested
- **Skills**: Validated and developing skills

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### "Path not found"
**Cause**: Path was deleted or you don't have access
**Solution**: 
- Check Path History for available paths
- Ask the path owner to share it again

#### "Session expired"
**Cause**: You were logged out
**Solution**:
- Click Login
- Enter your credentials
- Your progress is saved

#### "Test not found"
**Cause**: Test session expired (usually after 30 minutes)
**Solution**:
- Start a new test
- Your previous score is saved

#### Flashcards not saving
**Cause**: Network issue or browser cache
**Solution**:
- Refresh the page
- Check your internet connection
- Clear browser cache

#### Mobile layout broken
**Cause**: Browser zoom or old browser
**Solution**:
- Reset zoom to 100%
- Use Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge
- Update your browser

### Performance Tips

#### Faster Loading
- Close unused tabs
- Clear browser cache monthly
- Use a modern browser
- Check internet connection

#### Better Study Experience
- Use headphones for video resources
- Study in a quiet environment
- Take breaks every 25 minutes (Pomodoro)
- Use the focus timer

#### Offline Usage
- Download resources before going offline
- Flashcards work offline
- Study notes are cached
- Sync when back online

## Best Practices

### Learning
1. **Start with placement test** to find your level
2. **Study one phase at a time** (don't skip ahead)
3. **Use all resources**: videos, articles, projects
4. **Take notes** while learning
5. **Test yourself** after each phase
6. **Review missed questions** to learn from mistakes

### Collaboration
1. **Set realistic deadlines** (1-2 weeks per phase)
2. **Post regular check-ins** (weekly)
3. **Help teammates** when they're stuck
4. **Celebrate milestones** together

### Portfolio
1. **Complete projects** to build evidence
2. **Aim for 70%+ on tests** to validate skills
3. **Share your portfolio** with recruiters
4. **Update privacy settings** as you progress

## FAQ

**Q: Can I change my learning path?**
A: Yes! Generate a new path anytime. Your progress on the old path is saved.

**Q: How long does a path take?**
A: Depends on your pace. Most paths take 3-6 months at 2 hours/day.

**Q: Can I study offline?**
A: Partially. Flashcards and notes work offline. Sync when back online.

**Q: How do I share my progress?**
A: Export your portfolio as Markdown or share your public profile URL.

**Q: What if I fail a phase test?**
A: Take the remediation quiz to review missed topics, then retake the test.

**Q: Can I study with friends?**
A: Yes! Create a study group and invite them with the group code.

**Q: How do I export my path?**
A: Click the path, then **Export** → choose format (Markdown, Calendar, etc.)

**Q: Is my data private?**
A: Yes. Your portfolio is private by default. You control what's public.

## Glossary

- **Adaptive**: Adjusts difficulty based on your answers
- **Mastery**: Validated skill (70%+ test score)
- **Phase**: Major section of a learning path
- **Remediation**: Re-teaching of missed topics
- **Skill Passport**: Your public portfolio of skills
- **SM-2**: Spaced repetition algorithm for flashcards
- **Topic**: Specific concept within a phase
- **Validated**: Skill proven through testing
